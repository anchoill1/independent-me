📋 Parent Guide

🏠 Home Screen

Tap any routine card to open it. Use ↑ ↓ arrows in Settings to reorder them. Toggle routines on/off using the Show/Hide toggles.

✅ Doing a Routine

Tap the left side of a step to read it aloud. Tap the circle on the right to tick it done. Complete all steps to earn a ⭐ star and see the celebration!

🕐 Timer

Steps with a time (e.g. "2 minutes") show a 🕐 clock button. Tap it to start a countdown — a soft tick plays every second, switching to a more noticeable tick for the last 5 seconds, then it auto-ticks the step done and scrolls to the next step.

⭐ Stars & Rewards

Each completed routine earns a star. Set a goal and reward in Settings.

⚙️ Settings (PIN protected)

• Routine picker at top — tap to switch which routine you're editing
• Edit steps with ↑↓ arrows, add, delete, duplicate
• Set the days each step appears — tap the day chips (Su–Sa) under any step. For example, show "Have a shower" on Wednesday only. Steps automatically appear on the right days, and the routine shows today's day at the top
• Add custom routines below the steps editor
• Set reward goal, active days, star icon, child's name
• Reorder & show/hide routines (including custom ones) in the visibility card
• Edit Stars calendar — tap any day to add/remove a star manually
• Change PIN in the PIN card

📅 Different Steps on Different Days

Each step can appear only on the days you choose — ideal for school-day vs weekend routines. In Settings, under any step in the steps editor, tap the day chips (Su–Sa) to pick its days. For example: show "Swimming kit" on Tuesdays only, "Music practice" at weekends, or "Put the bins out" on Thursdays. Steps then appear automatically on the right days, and the banner at the top of the routine shows today’s day. Leave every day on (or none selected) and the step simply shows every day.

⏰ Screen Timeout

The app keeps the screen awake automatically. If the screen still times out, set Auto-Lock to Never: iPhone/iPad: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. Android: Settings → Display → Screen timeout → Never. The screen will go back to normal when the app is closed.

🔑 Setting & Recovering your PIN

The first time you open Settings you'll be asked to create a 4-digit PIN. After that, Settings always asks for it. You can change it any time in Settings → Change PIN.

If you forget your PIN, the recovery PIN 0000 always works — use it to get in, then set a new one.

If you need a full reset (clears all data including stars): open the app in your browser, go to Settings → Clear Site Data → Clear.

💾 Saving Data

Everything saves automatically on the device. Stars stay on the device they were earned on — they won't appear if you open the app on a different device or browser.

🔄 Backup & Transfer (move to another device)

To copy your routines to another phone or tablet: in Settings → Backup & Transfer, tap Export backup and copy the code (or save the file). On the other device, open the same card, tap Import backup, paste the code or open the file, and confirm.

This is a one-time snapshot, not live sync — if you change a routine later, export and import again to update the other device. Restoring replaces what is on that device (including your PIN), and stays fully offline — nothing is sent anywhere.
