Privacy Policy — Independent Me

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Independent Me is a visual routine app that helps children and adults with additional needs build independence one step at a time. It is provided by Clickeasy, Ireland.

What we collect

Nothing. Independent Me does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your routines, custom steps, PIN, and display settings (such as large text) directly on your own device. This information never leaves the device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is not accessible to us in any way. Uninstalling the app removes it.

PIN

The optional PIN is used only to stop a child changing settings on that device. It is stored on the device alone and is not transmitted anywhere.

Read-aloud voice

The app can read step text aloud using the text-to-speech voice already built into your device. This is handled by the device itself. No recording is made and no audio is sent anywhere.

Internet, advertising and analytics

The app works entirely offline. It makes no internet connections, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Children

Independent Me is designed for use by children and by adults with additional needs. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about children is gathered, stored, or shared.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
